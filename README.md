# 🚀 Zepto Sales Dashboard – Power BI Project

## 📘 Project Overview

The **Zepto Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI report that provides detailed insights into the sales, product performance, and stock availability of Zepto's 10-minute delivery app.  
It helps in analyzing product demand, stock shortages, and pricing trends to improve inventory planning and sales strategy.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze **total revenue**, **products sold**, and **average selling price**.
- Identify **out-of-stock products** and their categories.
- Track **discount patterns** and **price performance**.
- Discover **top-selling products** and **high-performing categories**.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|--------------|
| **Total Revenue** | ₹12 Billion total revenue generated |
| **Total Products Sold** | 796K items sold |
| **Number of Out-of-Stock Items** | 453 products currently out of stock |
| **Average Discount Price** | ₹7.62 per item |
| **Average Selling Price** | ₹14.19K per product |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### 🟣 1. Out-of-Stock Analysis
- Visualized using a **donut chart** to show the number of out-of-stock items by category.  
- **Top categories** with stock issues include *Cooking Essentials*, *Chocolates & Confectionery*, and *Munchies*.

### 🟢 2. Category by Quantity
- Shows product quantity distribution across categories.  
- Highlights high-demand items in *Ice Cream & Desserts* and *Packaged Foods*.

### 🔵 3. Top 10 Products by Quantity
- Displays leading brands like **Kellogg’s**, **MTR**, **Pedigree**, and **Ching’s** based on sales volume.

### 🟠 4. Category by Quantity (Bar Chart)
- Compares total revenue contribution across categories.  
- *Chocolates* and *Ice Creams* lead in total revenue.

### 🔴 5. MRP vs Discount Selling Price
- A scatter chart comparing product MRP with the actual discounted selling price.  
- Useful for monitoring pricing trends and discount effectiveness.

---

## 🧩 Filters Used
- **Category Filter** – View data for a specific product category.  
- **Product Name Filter** – Drill down into specific products.
